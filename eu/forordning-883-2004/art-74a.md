# Trygdeforordningen artikkel 74a

**The European Labour Authority**

**Kilde:** Trygdeforordningen (Forordning nr. 883/2004), EUR-Lex 02004R0883-20190731, språk EN.

Dette er EU-tekst, ikke norsk lov- eller forskriftstekst. Hvilken versjon som gjelder i Norge, avgjøres av EØS-avtalen og norsk gjennomføring: se `eu/forordning-883-2004/VEIVISER.md`.

---

1. Without prejudice to the tasks and activities of the Administrative Commission, the European Labour Authority shall support the application of this Regulation in accordance with its tasks set out in Regulation (EU) 2019/1149. The Administrative Commission shall cooperate with the European Labour Authority in order to coordinate the activities in mutual agreement and avoid any duplication. To that end, it shall conclude a cooperation agreement with the European Labour Authority.

2. The Administrative Commission may request the European Labour Authority to refer an issue concerning social security under mediation in accordance with the third subparagraph of Article 13(11) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1149.
